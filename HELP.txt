QUICK START:
------------
1. Right-click on the dock background to open settings:
   - Toggle Ball Animation [ON] / [OFF]
   - Change Ball Speed (e.g. 1.0, 1.5, 2.0)
   - Toggle Icon Hover Magnification [ON] / [OFF]
   - Change Hover Speed (e.g. 1.0, 1.5, 2.0)
   - Add new app or shortcut

2. Drag & Drop:
   - Drag any .exe or shortcut directly from your computer onto 
     the dock to automatically add it.
   - Click and drag icons left/right to rearrange their order.

3. App Options:
   - Right-click directly on any app icon to launch, change icon, 
     edit, or remove it.

4. Zero CPU:
   - When the cursor leaves the dock, the skin automatically 
     enters sleep mode (0% CPU).
